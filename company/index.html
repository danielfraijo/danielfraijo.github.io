About

An air-vehicle house, at concept.

Vespasian studies air vehicles across launch class. The public line is Fulmen, Pilum, Contus, Aquila, and Sagitta — air-launched, gun-launched, vertical-cell, recoverable, and container-launched. Each page carries a name, a stencil, geometry you can handle, and the analysis pack that belongs on it.

The work is digital engineering: parametric outer mold lines, force and heat, structures, and trajectory. Figures on these pages are concept until the solver export overwrites the file. The frame stays. That is what the site is for.

Seeker, payload, and anything that should not sit in the open stay off these pages.
