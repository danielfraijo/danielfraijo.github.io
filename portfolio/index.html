Compact powder-gun projectile, designed with the University of Arizona undergraduate team and Lockheed Martin. Axially symmetric ogive-fin, chosen over cone-flare, elliptical, and lifting-body classes on range, heating, cost, and launch survivability. Control is linear-actuated pins at the fin roots — authority without a hinge line under launch shock. Solids here are the working CAD: full-size pin family, 72% stingless and sting tunnel models, a no-fillet study, and design-table row 5.77. Public write-up stays at concept: OML, pins, trajectory, test path. No seeker. No payload layout.

Trajectory

Climb, hold, fall steep.

ICLOCS2 with a 9 km ceiling and a ≥70° terminal. Mach 5 and Mach 8 powder-gun launches. Pitch-over after the dense air, cruise, then a steep attack. No mid-course datalink — INS for the short flight.

Vehicle class

Why ogive-fin.

Four OMLs scored on range, heating, cost, and risk. Axially symmetric bodies stay cheaper to machine and cooler at Mach 8. Lifting bodies go farther and cost three times as much. Ogive-fin won the combined score.

Four regions, one limit.

No single alloy does stagnation, launch, and the 74 °C interior. Nose, cone, aft, fins, then Min-K throughout. Inner-wall temperatures from the 1-D stack sit under the electronics limit on the Mach 8 path.

Independent reconstruction · not a paper credit

SFRJ, revolved

Three-dimensional reconstruction of the simplified solid-fuel ramjet in Khokhar, McBeth, and Hanquist, AIAA SciTech 2025. The paper is theirs. The 3-D solid, the Pointwise mesh, and the SU2 check mesh are the replication.

The published case is a 2-D axisymmetric dump combustor: inlet, backward-facing step, grain wall treated as heat flux, symmetric converging-diverging nozzle. I rebuilt those stations as a SolidWorks revolve, meshed the 2-D half in SU2 (12,297 triangles) as a check against the paper's ~60k-cell domain, and built a separate 3-D Pointwise mesh of about 500,000 cells. The 3-D work asks whether the 2-D thrust-versus-heat-flux curve and the unstart at high wall heat still appear when the body is a real solid of revolution — not to replace the paper, and not to claim the analytical theory or the RCAC controller. Coefficients from the 3-D run stay off this page until they are cleaned. Geometry and mesh only.

Outer mold line only. Inlet, dump step, combustor, CD nozzle. No grain, no pylon, no injector.

Trajectory · ICLOCS2

Optimal-control trajectories for Mach 5 and Mach 8 powder-gun launch, with a 9 km ceiling and a steep terminal attack.

Status · Closed on the competition design

Design goals

  • Maximize downrange under altitude and impact-angle constraints
  • Feed AoA history back into CBAERO for trajectory-integrated heating
  • Keep static stability through muzzle exit and early ascent

Key results

  • Mach 8 max range 250 km · 196 s · 70°
  • Mach 5 max range 124 km · 132 s · 71°
  • Min range 25 km (M8) / 15 km (M5)

Aero and heating · SU2 / CBAERO

Coefficient tables and wall-temperature comparisons across Mach 2.5–8. RANS in SU2 against panel-method CBAERO, then heating along the optimized path into a 1-D conduction stack.

Status · Design set; tunnel campaign proposed

Design goals

  • Anchor drag and heating before committing the OML
  • Size the pin for trim and a 15 g case at 9 km
  • Keep inner-mold-line temperature under 74 °C

Key results

  • Max L/D 3.5 at Mach 5, α = 10°
  • Max L/D 3.7 at Mach 8, α = 10°
  • CD within ~2–21% of SU2 across the Mach ladder

SFRJ 3-D reconstruction · SU2 / Pointwise

Independent remesh and revolve of the Khokhar–McBeth–Hanquist dump-combustor case. Combustion still modeled as wall heat, as in the paper — not a chemistry claim.

Status · Geometry and meshes closed; 3-D coefficients not public yet

Write-ups

Reports

  • UCAH · University of Arizona / Lockheed Martin · 2025

    Hyper-Shot — 2025 Undergraduate Hypersonic Flight Design Competition

    Gun-launched ogive-fin projectile with pin-based control, sized for Mach 5–8 launch, 15 g at 9 km, and a steep terminal attack.

    Ogive-fin selected over lifting-body and elliptical classes on the combined range–heating–cost–risk score. Pins retained as the primary effector.

  • UCAH semi-finalist briefing · January 2026

    Hyper-Shot test campaign — CUBRC LENS and ARL gun launch

    Force and moment matrix for pin effectiveness at Mach 6–8, plus a free-flight gun-launch plan for sabot dynamics and static stability.

    Primary uncertainty is pin authority under shock-boundary-layer interaction. Wind-tunnel AoA sweeps come before the ARL shot.

  • Khokhar, McBeth, Hanquist · AIAA SciTech 2025 · DOI 10.2514/6.2025-0392

    Investigation of Solid Fuel Ramjets using Analytical Theory and Computational Fluid Dynamics

    Source paper · not mine

    Published UA case: analytical SFRJ thrust with NASA CEA, then a 2-D SU2 dump combustor with combustion modeled as wall heat. I did not write this paper. I rebuilt the geometry in 3-D to replicate the CFD setup.

    The paper reports a nonlinear thrust-versus-heat-flux curve and unstart near 12 MW/m². This page carries the revolved solid and the meshes only — not a restatement of their plots as mine.
